CYBERSECURITY AWARENESS

Stay Safe Online

Practical habits that reduce your exposure to phishing regardless of what any scanner tells you.

01
Check the domain
Always inspect the actual domain name before entering credentials — not just the page's appearance.
02
Don't trust urgency
Phishing messages often use fear, urgency, or account warnings to pressure quick action.
03
Verify through official channels
Instead of clicking a suspicious link, manually open the organization's official website or app.
04
Never share OTPs or passwords
Legitimate organizations won't ask for sensitive authentication details through a link.
05
Enable multi-factor authentication
MFA adds another layer of protection even if a password is compromised.
06
Keep software updated
Security updates patch known vulnerabilities attackers rely on.
